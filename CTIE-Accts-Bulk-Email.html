Stage 1 · The Python Engine

A local script does the heavy lifting

kle_invoice_processor.py — 555 lines that turn the raw PDF into branded, tracked, ledger-ready invoices in one command.

1

Extract

Reads every page with pdfplumber, parsing invoice number, buyer, GSTIN, sq.ft., taxable value, GST split, and outstanding balance — handling both the GST and NIL-tax invoice formats.

pdfplumberregex
2

De-duplicate

Checks each invoice number against the sheet before processing — re-running on the same file silently skips what's already there, so no double-sends.

idempotent
3

Split & Stamp

Each page becomes its own PDF, stamped with a hand-drawn green leaf, the Go-Green footer, a red heart signature, and a semi-transparent KLE-CTIE official seal.

pypdfreportlab
4

Upload

Stamped PDFs go to Google Drive via OAuth (owned by you, not a quota-less service account), each granted anyone-with-link read access for email recipients.

Drive APIOAuth
5

Populate

One row per invoice lands in a Google Sheet with branded headers, status dropdowns, and 27 columns spanning extraction data, tracking, and payment fields.

gspreadSheets API

Stage 2 · The Apps Script Engine

Branded email, sent & tracked

Every invoice goes out as a KLE-navy HTML card with a live CTA, an open-tracking pixel, and an automatic reminder schedule — all running inside the Sheet for free.

Branded Send

HTML invoice card with summary, bank details, and a "View & Download" CTA linking to the Drive PDF.

Open & Click Tracking

A 1×1 pixel logs opens; the CTA routes through the web app to log clicks — both written back to the sheet.

Auto Follow-ups

A daily 9 AM trigger sends Reminder 1 at Day 7 and Reminder 2 at Day 15 — only for unpaid invoices.

Payment Receipt

Marking an invoice paid fires a green acknowledgement email with the payment mode and reference number.

The architecture in one line

A local Python script feeds a Google Sheet database. Apps Script reads that sheet to send branded email, track engagement, and run scheduled reminders — while a single-page portal serves as the human interface. The Sheet is the single source of truth; everything else is a lens onto it.

One command.
The rest takes care of itself.

Drop the monthly PDF, run the script, and open the portal. Invoices are split, stamped, uploaded, emailed, tracked, reminded, and reconciled — end to end.
